TipCalc Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

TipCalc is a calculator application developed by penningtonProgramming. It provides basic calculator functionality and quick tip calculations.

Information We Collect

TipCalc does not require user registration or request personal information to perform calculations.

Calculator Data

Bill amounts, calculations, and tip percentages are processed locally on your device. TipCalc does not transmit these calculations to our servers.

Advertising and Analytics

TipCalc does not include advertising or developer-added analytics services.

Third-Party Services

The Android version is distributed through Google Play and uses Capacitor to provide Android functionality. Google Play and Android services may process information independently under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

TipCalc is a general-purpose calculator and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if TipCalc's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be posted here.

Contact
